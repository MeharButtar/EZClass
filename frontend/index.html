How it works

  1. 1

    Pick your major

    Choose it once from the list. You instantly see every class it requires: prep courses, major courses, pick-one choices and electives, with the this term's sections under each class.

  2. 2

    Add sections

    Point at (or tap) a section to preview it on your week. If it clashes with something you already added, it turns red before you add it. Tap + Add when it fits.

  3. 3

    Fill in your GEs

    Open the GE page. Each GE area has its own list, and areas your major classes already cover are marked, so you don't take a class you don't need.

  4. 4

    Check and compare

    My Schedule shows your full week, runs a conflict check, and keeps up to three plans (A, B and C) so you have a backup when a class fills up.

Why it's easier

  • Everything for your major on one page. No more jumping between the catalog and the class schedule to work out what you still need.
  • Check professors before you pick. Every professor's name links to a RateMyProfessors search in a new tab, so you can look up their reviews without losing your plan.
  • Conflicts caught as you go. Overlaps show up in red while you plan, not when registration opens.
  • Mark what you've already taken. Tick "I took this" and your progress for each requirement updates.
  • No account, no sign-up. Your plans stay in your own browser.
  • Made for your phone too. Plan between classes; your week opens as a sheet at the bottom of the screen.
